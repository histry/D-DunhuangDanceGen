# R40 deliverables and limits

The 268,160-parameter Context module is the only trained generation component. Matched and Extra each perform 240 updates, on identical case/batch schedules, with actual-state refreshes at steps 0 and 120. A matched update runs both official CFG branches; Extra runs one conditional forward. Equal updates and parameter count do not mean equal FLOPs. Both evaluated arms use the same R39 reverse process and frozen RTS.

A separate SDMR experiment performs 80 updates from the original R38 initialization seed, preserving its full-unit global multi-positive objective. A local task independently crops 90 frames of music and motion, with origins in [0,30]. Only the true recording clock constructs labels a+i=b+j; the encoder receives neither origin. Interior queries [30,60), equal lengths and common observed-channel masks are shared across all offset/zero/swap controls. Five controlled origins are evaluated for every eligible Val unit, with tie-uniform accuracy. This is physical-clock recovery, not event semantic truth.

Continuous ChangE preparation recovers observed 22-joint body and relative fitted Root from real single recordings. Four recording UIDs are registered for future adaptation and four held from that adaptation, with known Feitian/Pipa learning controls distinguished. Early/middle/late intervals are metadata-fixed. The Root gauge uses the first visible frame and the frozen model box midpoint; unsupported clips are reported and never clipped or silently dropped. No fake music, contact or shaped world-foot target is assigned. This dataset is preflight only; target-domain training and a Source-gate comparison are separate future experiments, not represented as completed here.

The main comparison is a combined execution-contract intervention. Its outcome cannot attribute gains or failures separately to CFG, state refresh, projected losses, or derivative terms. The Source gate and routing are explicitly unchanged. No whole-song or default-path qualification is granted.
